    About

    Breath Slow is a simple breathing practice app that opens directly in your browser with no installation or sign-up. It is made for moments when you need to slow down, steady your breath, and reduce extra stimulation. All settings are saved only on your device, and the interface can be adjusted to your needs: rhythm, visuals, music, and accessibility options. This is a supportive self-help tool, not a medical service.
